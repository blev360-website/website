Questions

FAQ

How much does it cost?

Pricing depends on your site, how many dumpsters you have, and your pickup schedule, so we quote each site individually. Most customers save because they need fewer hauls. Your first crunch is always free, so you can see the difference before you pay anything. Book online or call (720) 973-1391 for a free quote.

How much space does compaction free up?

Up to 80% of the dumpster's capacity, depending on what's in it. Bulky, loose material like wood, cardboard, and construction debris compacts the most.

How many crunches before a haul?

Most customers get 1–2 crunches before they need a haul. Every crunch that pushes a haul back is a haul fee you don't pay yet.

Do I have to switch haulers?

No. If you like your current hauler, keep them and just add crunching. If you'd rather hand it off, we can handle hauling too, so you get crunching and hauling from one company on one bill.

How does your hauling work?

We work with a partner hauling company that gives us solid rates. We pass those savings on, so your haul costs are usually lower than arranging hauling on your own. You deal only with us for scheduling and billing.

What materials can you compact?

Pretty much anything. We'll tell you if it's not a good fit.
