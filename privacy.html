Privacy Policy

How Dumpster Crunchers LLC collects, uses, and protects your information.

✉ hauling@dumpstercrunchers.com
Effective Date This Privacy Policy is effective as of the date posted below and applies to all visitors, customers, and users of dumpstercrunchers.com and Dumpster Crunchers LLC's services.
Section 01

Who We Are

Dumpster Crunchers LLC (“Dumpster Crunchers,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) is a Colorado limited liability company providing mobile dumpster compaction and related waste hauling coordination services in the Denver Metro area. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, disclose, and protect information we receive through our website, phone system, and business operations.

Section 02

Information We Collect

We may collect the following categories of information:

  • Contact Information: Name, business name, phone number, email address, and service/property address, when you request a quote, book a demo, or become a customer.
  • Service and Billing Information: Details about the services provided to you, invoicing history, and payment information processed through our scheduling and invoicing platform.
  • Communications: Records of calls, texts, and emails exchanged with us regarding scheduling, service requests, or support.
  • Website Usage Data: Information automatically collected when you visit our website, including pages viewed, general location (city/region level), device and browser type, and referral source, collected via Google Analytics.
  • Form Submissions: Information you submit through website contact or quote request forms.
Section 03

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Schedule, coordinate, and provide compaction and hauling services;
  • Communicate with you about appointments, service updates, and billing;
  • Process payments and maintain accurate business and accounting records;
  • Respond to inquiries submitted through our website or by phone;
  • Improve our website and understand how visitors use it;
  • Comply with legal, tax, and regulatory obligations.
Section 04

Third-Party Services We Use

We rely on the following third-party service providers to operate our business, each of which may process information on our behalf subject to their own privacy practices:

  • Scheduling, Invoicing & CRM Platform: Used to manage customer accounts, scheduling, invoicing, and payment processing.
  • Google Analytics: Used to understand website traffic and visitor behavior in aggregate.
  • Website Form Provider: Used to process and route website contact/quote request form submissions to our email.
  • Google Workspace: Used for business email and related communications.
  • Business Phone Provider: Used to manage business calls and text messages.

We do not control the privacy practices of these third-party providers, and we encourage you to review their respective privacy policies.

Section 05

Cookies and Tracking Technologies

Our website may use cookies and similar tracking technologies (such as those used by Google Analytics) to collect information about how visitors use our site. You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings; however, doing so may affect certain website functionality.

Section 06

We Do Not Sell Your Information

Dumpster Crunchers does not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for their own marketing purposes. Information is shared only with the service providers described in Section 04 as necessary to operate our business, or as required by law.

Section 07

Data Retention

We retain customer and business information for as long as necessary to provide our services, maintain accurate business and tax records, and comply with applicable legal obligations. Website analytics data is retained according to the retention settings of our analytics provider.

Section 08

Data Security

We use commercially reasonable administrative and technical safeguards to protect the information we collect. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Section 09

Your Choices

You may contact us at any time to ask what information we hold about you, to request corrections, or to request that we delete information that is not otherwise required to be retained for legal, tax, or business record-keeping purposes. To make a request, email hauling@dumpstercrunchers.com.

Section 10

Children's Privacy

Our services and website are directed to businesses and adults, and are not intended for individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Section 11

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for other operational, legal, or regulatory reasons. The updated version will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
